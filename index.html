Flora Survey

Record species, polygons and field notes on site. Everything saves on this phone as you go.

  1. Start a survey. Enter the site and date. Your name goes on everything you record.
  2. Record as you walk. Search and add species, draw polygons on the aerial map, and add field notes and photos. It all saves on this phone automatically, even with no signal.
  3. Survey together (optional). Start a live survey and share the code, or join a teammate's. Everyone sees the same species list.
  4. Finish. Tap Finish survey. It's filed under Open a saved survey, and you can Export it (Excel, GIS and photos) to send on.

How team sync works

Everyone sees the same list as species are added. If two of you log the same (or a similar) species, both entries are kept, each with its own points, photos and notes. Photos stay on each person's own phone and are combined when you export.
